About these recordings & measurements

Each audio layer is the same 24-second passage, starting 30 seconds into its song. Excerpts are encoded from retained production WAVs without separate loudness normalization, so levels can differ. “Tony solo” is the assembled converted vocal; “Finished mix” comes from the delivered recording. The complete song uses its existing release MP3.

Stage intervals include setup and any work between the recorded boundaries. Vocal timing and ending checks sit with separation; the Tony stage includes pitch preparation and conversion; checks include any vocal retry. Publishing includes the final mix, export and upload. No new songs or paid calls were made for this tour.
